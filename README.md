Welcome to the repo. This is the track of my regular documentation of the work done by me during my research internship at IIT Kanpur. Feel free to follow my methodologies as I learnt working in the lab or upgrade the existing framework adding your own customisation from subjective process parameters.

The pipeline developed eventually was meant to be coupled with an AR welding simulator, complementing it and together deployed in the lab to be used as an educational tool for the undergrads to develop and understanding of the physics behind the solidification phenomenon of metallic substrates.
