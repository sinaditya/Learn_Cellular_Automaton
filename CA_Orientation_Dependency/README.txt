Here, we will incorporate all the things namely, growth in the direction of thermal gradient, growth proportional to the intensity of temperature differential and the new growth dependant on orientation of boundary as well. This now simulates the effect of crystallography.
Each grain is now associated with a specific local orientation. Grains aligned with the heat flow will grow faster whereas grains misaligned with it will be very slow to expand.
Note: true crystal physics, dendrite arm geometry, interface curvature, etc., are still not modelled yet. Instead, we will now include affect on orientation on the growth probability.
